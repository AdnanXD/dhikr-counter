Dhikr Counter: free tasbih and zikr counter

Dhikr Counter is a free tasbih and zikr counter for your phone. It has no ads, no accounts and no tracking, your count stays on your device, and it works offline. It includes nine dhikr presets from Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim.

  • Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh (Sahih al-Bukhari 6384, Sahih Muslim 2704)
  • The four most beloved words (Sahih Muslim 2137 & 2695)
  • Morning tasbīḥ of Juwayriyah (Sahih Muslim 2726)
