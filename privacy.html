Privacy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Dhikr Counter collects no personal data.

What the app does not have

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics and no tracking.
  • No ads.
  • No cookies.
  • No third-party services of any kind.

Where your counts are kept

Your counts and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your own device. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing the site data for mydhikr.app in your browser removes them.

Network requests

The app makes no network requests except loading itself. After the first visit it can run offline.

The Android app

The Android app is the same web app, so everything on this page applies to it as well.

Children

Dhikr Counter is not directed at children.
